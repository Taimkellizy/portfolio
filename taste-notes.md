## Design DNA (synthesize, never copy)

1. Type is the architecture — giant display words as layout structure + tiny caps metadata
2. Grain is a material — dither/grainy gradients as surfaces and section breaks
3. Floating rotated photo drift — scatter-and-drift parallax is the signature motion candidate
4. Marquee strips + four-point star glyphs (authored SVG, never Unicode) as separators
5. Dark immersive base (user pin: dark only, no white bg) with grainy gradient moments
6. Circular ↗ buttons, hairline row lists, accordion detail blocks

## Calibration warns (avoid unless the world earns them)

Warm cream + terracotta + high-contrast serif; near-black + one neon accent + glowing edges; broadsheet hairlines + italic display + tracked mono labels. The taste refs contain glow (OXALEY) and grainy gradient (folk/starburst) — those are pinned brief materials, not defaults.

## What the 5 rejected explorations got wrong

Generic mood skins, no product truth, decorative effects over structural design, template skeletons. The new concepts must show the real portfolio working — real facts, real hierarchy — inside each world.
